A star chart of 245 terms in 11 constellations, plus 53 field stars. Mined from 1,142 GitHub repos and grouped by resonance, with 130 new words measured across 6,784 more repos.

## How to read it

- A line between two terms means resonance: they appear in the same repos more often than chance.
- Color means tier. Grounded is an established field or working method. Speculative is serious but unproven. Practice covers contemplative, symbolic or creative traditions, judged by use rather than truth claims. Fringe conflicts with established science or cannot be tested as stated.
- Line types: measured (co-occurrence), stated (gold dotted, declared connections), translations (cyan, systems terms paired with soul-side terms), rhymes (red dotted, curated groupings).
- Starred terms are new words from the frontier measurement. Gold terms are coined terms.
- Field stars resonate with nothing yet: too rare in these repos for a pattern to form.

## Method

Terms were extracted from READMEs and docs across 1,142 public repos, then clustered by co-occurrence measured against chance (modularity 0.659). A second pass measured 130 new words across 6,784 more repos. Curated constellations (the Extraction, the Inverted Prism, the Temple) are labeled as curated. Coined terms appear only when someone else's repo uses them in the same sense, with labeled exceptions. The full method, layers and sources are in `research-report.md`. Snapshots go stale; re-mining on a cadence is what keeps a map like this live.
