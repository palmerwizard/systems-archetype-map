**Measured (441):** two terms share GitHub repos at least 1.5 times as often as chance would give. The map has three layers, and the page labels each one:

That's the Extraction, the Inverted Prism, the Temple, the orbiting stars and the centre column. **Interpreted:** readings with sources. That's the translations, the stated links, the trajectories and the third position.

## 2. The column, top to bottom

**The Lens, the Prism and the Loom** make up the centre row.

**The Prism** (interoception, metacognition, cognitive sovereignty, anti-deskilling) is the third position between the Lens (the formal sciences) and the Loom (systems language). **Tiferet orbits the Prism** as its counterpart on the Tree of Life: the heart of the middle pillar, where mercy and severity are reconciled. Gevurah (severity) orbits it on the side facing the Inverted Prism; war as Gevurah unbalanced.

**The literature lands in the same place.** Integration of opposites shows up as: Jung's transcendent function and union of opposites; Nicholas of Cusa's coincidence of opposites; Linehan's DBT dialectic; Barry Johnson's polarity management.

### The Temple: the bridge.

What's in it: temple dynamics (coined term); the other temples: Jung's temenos, Durkheim's collective effervescence, Turner's communitas, Bion's basic assumption groups, and TempleOS; the confessional hinge. **Rosetta's line:** "Archons are the mythic layer of the extraction story; enclosure is the systems layer; the temple is where they meet." **Evidence:** TempleOS is the term with real GitHub weight, at 1,249 repos (587 since 2025). The other temples appear on GitHub mostly as names, not in this sense.

**The confessional hinge** (now inside the Temple):

**The reading:** held by the observer, the aggregate of what people confess feeds the Inverted Prism. Returned to the people who confessed it, it feeds the Prism. Which way it's returned decides which. **Returned examples:** PostSecret; the #MeToo disclosure study: 1.8 million tweets, where seeing others disclose made people share more and reduced stigma; data donation; differential privacy, Dwork's method for returning the aggregate without exposing anyone in it. **Analysed, with only top-level findings published:** Anthropic's Clio and the NBER study of ChatGPT use. **Speculative note:** the confessional cluster is where zero-point knowledge gets traded. *Documented:* confession reconstructed from behaviour is sold. Duke Sanford found data brokers selling mental-health data, and the FTC sued Kochava over sensitive location data. *Not documented:* that what people tell AI is traded. **On GitHub:** confessional clustering was the only coined term to pass the outside-match test, through one repo that clusters Reddit confessions.

**The Inverted Prism**: the watcher, which takes everything in and gives nothing back, "except possibly safety from big threats."

**Systems layer:** the NSA's PRISM program under FISA Section 702 (FISA Amendments Act 2008, Protect America Act 2007, the 2023 PCLOB report); security theater (Schneier); cognitive warfare (NATO's 2021 report; the EJIL: Talk! analysis of whether it counts as prohibited force); the military-industrial complex (Eisenhower's 1961 farewell address). **Myth layer:** the all-seeing eye; Argus; the Gnostic Archons (from the Secret Book of John); Enoch's Watchers (the Grigori); the Qliphoth, the inverted Tree. Thagirion is the inverse of Tiferet, and Golachab the inverse of Gevurah. **Whistleblower cases:** Mark Klein (Room 641A, 2006); Snowden (PRISM, 2013); Wylie (Cambridge Analytica, 87 million profiles, 2018); Haugen (Facebook's internal research, 2021). **Documented times the watchers reached for the esoteric:** Stargate (remote viewing, 1978-95). The 1995 AIR evaluation found no operational value. MKUltra (1953-73). It was exposed in the 1977 Senate hearings, not vindicated. Vallely and Aquino's "From PSYOP to MindWar" (1980). Aquino had founded the Temple of Set. Jack Parsons, who co-founded JPL and led an Ordo Templi Orientis lodge. **What the record shows:** the watchers mostly came back empty-handed. One reading is that these myths are how cultures describe being watched by a power nobody can hold to account.

**The Extraction**: at the bottom, curated by rhyme with the extraction-systems map.

Its six moves, in order:

1. regulatory capture
2. technofeudalism
3. ethics washing
4. firehose of falsehood
5. algorithmic management

**What gets taken:** extractivism, surveillance capitalism, dark patterns, enshittification. **The confession line:** self-disclosure, which now hands off into the Temple. **War:** the same playbook at state scale. **Counter-moves:** sousveillance and platform cooperativism. **Collapsed terms:** near-duplicates were folded into one term per move. For example, self-disclosure now covers the privacy paradox, context collapse and ephemeral messaging.

## 10. Method and limits

**Searches:** public GitHub search, run one at a time, with exact-phrase queries only. A second search counts repos created since 1 January 2025 (70% for all of GitHub). **Coined terms** go on the map only when someone else's repo uses them in the same sense. Two were read by hand and accepted: mirror moment, somatic engineering. Temple dynamics was added by override and labeled as coined. **Placement:** measured constellations come from community detection; the Extraction, Inverted Prism and Temple are curated; everyday work, kundalini and the frisson cluster were placed by hand or by the centre of their sourced links, and each panel says so. Broad or ambiguous words count a link only when the repo also uses the map's vocabulary ("in-context repos"); where none passed, the raw results were kept, so some measured links may include other senses. **The public export** removes the follow graph, the named people, the cases and the organisations, and credits "rosetta". **Limits:** Measured links show that terms appear together in repos, not that one causes the other. The corpus is public GitHub repos, which skews toward the Engine: people who push code write about AI tooling, while sound practice, contemplation and temple work live largely off GitHub, so the Lyre and the Witness will always look thinner than they are. Some GitHub counts include repos that only share the name (Temenos, for one, is mostly a banking platform). The node list was chosen; modularity says the clusters are not noise given those nodes, not that the selection was neutral. Every curated group is labelled as curated. Thin evidence is shown, not hidden: union busting has 1 repo, company town 11, ASMR 7 in context, somatic experiencing 5, implicate order 1 in context. Repos that share only a name are rejected by hand where found (Indra's net, holographic universe). **Similar maps that already exist:** Anvaka's Map of GitHub; Crawford and Joler's Anatomy of an AI System and Calculating Empires; the CCRU Numogram; Crowley's 777 tables of correspondence; Mapa de la Conciencia. The map combines measured term resonance with curated esoteric and systems readings.
